Hi, I'm Danita Briggs. I provide personalized emotional, physical, and informational support for families during pregnancy, birth, and the postpartum period.

Birth Doula Support

Continuous physical, emotional, and informational support before, during, and just after birth.

  • Prenatal visits to build your birth plan
  • On-call availability from 38 weeks
  • Continuous labor support
  • Immediate postpartum care

Postpartum Doula Support

Nurturing care for the family during the "fourth trimester" to help you heal and adjust.

  • Newborn care and soothing techniques
  • Infant feeding support
  • Light household tasks and meal prep
  • Emotional support and processing
